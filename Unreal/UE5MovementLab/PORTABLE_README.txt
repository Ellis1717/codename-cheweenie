Codename .... Cheweenie — Windows core adventure 0.15.1

Double-click PLAY_GAME.cmd to start. Keep the entire Windows folder beside it.
Choose Play / continue adventure to explore the house and neighbourhood.

Move: WASD or the left stick. Look: mouse/right stick.
Interact: E / Xbox X / PlayStation Square. Jump: Space / Y / Triangle.
Pause: Escape / Menu / Options. Camera: V / right-stick press.
Use the in-game Controls guide for tricks, pet actions and current bindings.
Both sticks have a 20% dead zone to ignore small idle signals. LB + A/B/X/Y performs lie/roll/shake/bark; a short LB tap barks.
Pause > Controls > Screen overlays lets you hide individual gameplay overlays.

This cumulative release includes home progression, clearer brick/mortar and coat materials, natural pet voices, saved overlay looks, Rainbow Guy and his bite squeak, downward digging, walkable upstairs rooms, optional jackets and the controller-aware guide.
Earlier versions stay available through the local archive catalog, when supplied.
The portable ZIP excludes the owner's private map, saved progress and archive paths.
Physical controller handling and separate-PC LAN require human checks.

Controller comfort: click L3 to toggle hurry; A still works as hold-to-hurry. Use the left stick in menus. D-pad left/right zooms in solo; hold RB with D-pad left/right to zoom in rooms. Controls > Controller comfort setup saves camera speed/response, separate stick dead zones and L3 mode.

Solo Marty movement: Y/Triangle or Space jumps while moving/running. Hold LB/L1 + L3 to toggle crawl, or + R3 to toggle two-leg walking. Keyboard Z/U offers the same stances; they are also in the Tricks menu. Run returns to four paws when headroom permits. Crawl uses a lower collision capsule; blocked stand-up/jump attempts stay low. These transient solo stances reset at home/restart; LAN retains its existing pet-room controls.

Community sports park: visit the park and follow the sports sign. E / X / Square kicks, tee-bats or shoots. Hurry with Shift / L3 through bases 1, 2, 3 and HOME within 25 seconds. Reset bollards retain temporary scores. These are solo practice games.
