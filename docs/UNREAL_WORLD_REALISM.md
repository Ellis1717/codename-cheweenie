# Codename .... Cheweenie — World realism 0.10

This is an original environment-art pass on the actual playable room and neighbourhood. It improves the scenery's physical detail and lighting; it does not finish the pets' realism, scan the owner's home, or turn the schematic neighbourhood into a surveyed architectural reconstruction.

## Visible changes

The room has a complete architectural shell and ceiling, staggered oak floorboards, painted skirting/cornices, framed windows, pleated curtains, a panelled blue door and a cushioned four-seat sofa with separate pillows and edge piping. The woven rug has a border and fringe. The side table, books, plant, lamp and original abstract wall reliefs add scale and surface variety. Panelled cabinets, a sink/tap, brushed fridge and TV surround replace the visible kitchen placeholder blocks. The blanket fort uses a sagging cloth mesh with gathered sides and timber supports.

The exterior adds brick, slate, concrete, asphalt, grass and painted surfaces at world-space texture scales. Headquarters roof pitches now form gables, with infill rather than open floating roof planes. House/school/bay windows have opaque reflective glazing and batched trim. Park trees have connected branches/twigs and thin serrated leaves; a slatted bench replaces the old block. One 640-instance grass batch provides near-field ground detail with 12–20 metre culling. A distant ground backdrop reduces the immediate map-edge void. Homes, neighbour dogs and the school still have simplified forms; vegetation does not simulate wind.

The pass uses 12 original FBX meshes, a retained Blender scene, 21 base materials, eight world-space variants and 51 original base/normal/roughness maps. Oak maps are 2048 square; other maps are 1024. Surface normal strength is restrained and weighted upholstery normals are retained. Base colour uses sRGB; normal/roughness data is linear. Window daylight and the lamp use explicit candela units tuned for this project's fixed exposure, not measured household light levels.

## Play and retain earlier versions

PLAY_WORLD.cmd launches the independently gated World package; the desktop World shortcut targets that package. Its portable PLAY_WORLD.cmd also works after extracting the complete distribution folder on another Windows PC. For LAN play use the Host/Join scripts inside the same World folder on both PCs. The existing solo 0.9 and LAN 0.1 packages/launchers remain available. LAN still supports the indoor room only; outdoor travel and solo progression are not networked.

LocalGameData is optional and private. A local World launcher may read streets.json and NeighbourDogs.json from that package's LocalGameData folder; these files and runtime Saved folders must be excluded from any transfer archive. Without them, solo uses the public schematic neighbourhood. Standard keyboard and Xbox/PlayStation mappings are retained. Controller checks in automated reports are synthetic; physical devices and two separate PCs require separate verification.

`-CheweenieLegacyWorld` bypasses the detailed room/fort/material/vegetation overlays. Original Unity, comparison content, smooth/detailed Marty sources and private photo references are retained. New decorative meshes disable actor and component collision; the tested floor, sofa, room boundaries and gameplay interaction locations remain separate. Kitchen render replacements retain their original solid blockers. Decorative actors do not tick; grass and window trim use instanced batches.

## Rebuild and validation

Run BUILD_WORLD_ART.ps1 with Blender 5, IMPORT_WORLD.ps1, then BUILD.ps1. The authoring scene and all generators remain in SourceArt/WorldRealism and Scripts; no paid/downloaded art or copied background imagery is required. Generator hashes and mesh/material roles are recorded in manifest.json. Import compares source texture hashes so changed procedural maps cannot silently reuse stale imports. See [source notes](../Unreal/UE5MovementLab/SourceArt/WorldRealism/README.md).

TEST_WORLD.ps1 -Render runs the real map at 1920×1080, checks asset presence, coordinate alignment, collision separation, colour-space rules, keyboard traversal, door travel, grass batching, recovery and a synthetic controller camera binding. It records room, player-camera, headquarters and park screenshots. Room/home/park presentation shots use a scripted in-engine camera with HUD hidden; WorldDogView is the live player camera/HUD. These are rendered gameplay-world captures, not concept art. Offscreen frame-interval samples are limited diagnostics, not GPU timings, a before/after comparison or an interactive FPS guarantee.

Package to a new UnrealWorld-0.10.0 folder using PACKAGE.ps1 -OutputRoot. Against that executable, run TEST_WORLD -Render, TEST_COOP headless/rendered, TEST_COMPANIONS, TEST_ADVENTURE and TEST_LIFE into World/Headless/Rendered/Solo/Adventure/Life subfolders under a fresh test root. Run the solo editor movement regression because the room initialization is shared. FINALIZE_WORLD.ps1 validates fresh reports, zero runtime Error/Fatal/material fallbacks, four full-HD readable captures and payload hashes before enabling the World launcher. It also retains the LAN gate. Reports and actual final outcomes belong in evidence/ue5-world-build.json.

Final results: native Editor/Game builds and full cook/archive passed. The packaged world (14), headless/rendered co-op (45 each), companion play/reload (34), patrol/reload (31) and cozy actions/reload (28) passed, totaling 197 public packaged assertions. Four private-map checks and 16 editor movement checks also passed. Four full-HD captures were inspected and passed readability thresholds; final runtime Error/Fatal/material fallback counts were zero. All 43 payload hashes matched inside the 530,955,756-byte transfer ZIP. Windows PowerShell 5.1 verified the World/LAN launch gates and rejected an invalid join address. [Actual evidence](evidence/ue5-world-build.json).

Development findings repaired during this pass include the first enclosed-room lighting being too dim, the HQ roof slopes pointing outward, a flipped FBX Y axis, decorative collision not reporting the expected disabled state (now disabled at actor and component level), a grounded check occurring immediately after a test respawn, initial disconnected leaf geometry, unused Blender materials being absent after reload, and procedural maps needing hash-driven reimport. Physical controller feel, two-PC networking and a broad performance profile remain unverified. Offscreen samples were collected while a separate headless acceptance suite was running.
