# Rainbow Guy — Marty's favorite toy

The owner's November 3, 2026 photograph establishes **Rainbow Guy** as Marty's favorite toy. The native Unreal adventure adds an original, stylized plush monster with red/orange/yellow/green shaggy bands, blue horns, hands and feet, large eyes and a toothy grin. The manufacturer label is omitted. Geometry uses engine primitives and the project's existing surface material; no paid assets or third-party code are needed.

Find Rainbow Guy on the floor at home, beside the original ball. Use **E / Xbox X / PlayStation Square** to pick him up and **Q / LT / L2** to drop him. The existing backpack and dig/bury/recover actions work with him too. Pickup text identifies him as Marty's favorite, and mouth/backpack readouts use his name.

**He squeaks when bitten.** Picking him up or retrieving him from the backpack puts him in Marty's mouth and plays a short, high-pitched squeak. Dropping, storing and simply carrying him do not repeat the bite squeak. This is an original synthesized approximation using the retained toy chirp at 1.5× pitch, rather than a recording of the real toy. Effects volume and the sound-effects feature switch both mute it.
